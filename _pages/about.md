Hi! I'm **Suleman Munawar**, a Computer Engineer with an **MS in Computational Science and Engineering from NUST, Pakistan**. My work combines **machine learning, data analysis, and communication-network research**, with particular experience in **vehicular edge computing, Age of Information (AoI), Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS), and DNN-based computational offloading**.

I completed an **Erasmus+ research internship at Frederick University, Cyprus**, where I worked on Age of Information optimization in RIS-assisted vehicular networks. My research experience has contributed to publications in **IEEE Vehicular Technology Conference (VTC)**, **IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology**, and **IWCMC**.

Alongside research, I have professional experience in **data analytics, digital operations, front-end development, and software quality assurance**. My work has included developing dashboards and performance reports, analyzing data, maintaining digital systems, testing software, and preparing technical documentation.

My technical experience includes **Python, C++, machine learning, data analysis, deep neural networks, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**. I am particularly interested in technical roles involving **data analytics, machine learning, applied AI, research engineering, Python, and intelligent communication systems**.

Outside work and research, I enjoy playing piano 🎹, sketching 🖼️, skateboarding 🛹, riding motorcycles 🏍️, and following motorsports 🏎️💨.
